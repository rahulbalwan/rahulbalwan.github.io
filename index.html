About

Statistics for health research, with emphasis on careful interpretation.

I completed an MSc in Medical Statistics & Health Data Science at the University of Bristol, following postgraduate training in Statistics at IIT Kanpur and undergraduate training in Statistics at the University of Delhi.

My strongest areas are regression modelling, survival analysis, epidemiological methods, model diagnostics and reproducible statistical workflows. I am particularly interested in work where statistical methodology, real-world health data and multidisciplinary research meet.
